## Overview

**Electronics City’s Premier Retail Destination**

M5 Ecity Mall stands as Electronics City’s first integrated shopping and lifestyle destination, thoughtfully developed to combine **premium retail, dining, entertainment, and community-driven experiences** under one roof.

---

## Key Highlights & Features

- **India’s First Dine-in Theater Concept**  
  A unique cinematic experience complemented by a wide range of cuisines.

- **Dedicated Family Entertainment Centre (FEC)**  
  Spanning around **17,500 sq. ft.**, designed to deliver a fun-filled and engaging experience for kids and families.

- **Expansive F&B Offerings**  
  Featuring **34 brands** with multiple cuisine options and fine dining destinations spread across **28,522.70 sq. ft.** area, delivering a truly blissful experience.

- **Home to Leading Anchor Brands**  
  Offering top-quality products across an expansive **1,31,420 sq. ft.** of retail space.
